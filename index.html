What is Re:Struct?

  • A web application where you can experience capturing images, reconstructing point clouds, and scoring accuracy in real time.
  • Accuracy metrics such as RMSE is automatically computed against a ground truth point cloud.
  • Point clouds are visualized with Three.js, allowing free rotation to intuitively inspect where discrepancies occur.
  • Shooting conditions, reconstruction accuracy, and completion time are reflected in the final score, which determines the leaderboard.
